## Description (4000 chars max — edit before submitting)

Stay on top of every assignment, project, and deadline with Assignment Planner — the simple, focused tracker built for students.

**Add assignments in seconds**
Quickly log any assignment with a name, due date, course, and priority. A rolling date picker makes scheduling fast and error-free.

**See what's next at a glance**
Assignments sort by due date so your most urgent work is always at the top. A "work on next" banner highlights the one item that matters most right now.

**Calendar view**
Switch to the calendar to see your whole month at a glance. Dots mark days with assignments; tap any day to filter down to just what's due.

**Recurring assignments**
Set up weekly recurrences for ongoing tasks — lab reports, reading responses, weekly problem sets — and let the app generate every instance automatically.

**Smart reminders**
Get a notification the day before anything is due so nothing sneaks up on you.

**Sync across devices**
Sign in with your email and your assignments follow you across every iPhone and iPad you own.

**Simple and private**
No ads. No social features. No tracking beyond what you need to sync your own data.
